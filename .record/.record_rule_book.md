# Executive Policy EP-001

## Company Record Keeping Standard (CRKS v1.0)

**Status:** Approved

**Effective Immediately**

Every employee, manager, AI agent, and department must maintain complete records of their work. Every significant action must be documented in the `.record/` directory in a standardized, chronological, and auditable format.

This policy applies to:

* Human team members
* AI employees
* Executive decisions
* Technical work
* Product discussions
* Financial activities
* Emails
* Chats
* Meetings
* Daily task logs

---

# Objectives

* Maintain a complete project history.
* Enable auditability and accountability.
* Simplify onboarding and knowledge transfer.
* Preserve institutional knowledge.
* Support investor due diligence.

Employee ID:

Department:

Time: 09:35:42 UTC

# Mandatory Rules

Every employee and AI agent **must**:

1. Record every assigned task.
2. Record task status changes.
3. Record task completion.
4. Record important chats.
5. Record emails sent or received.
6. Record meetings attended.
7. Record executive approvals.
8. Record design decisions.
9. Record technical decisions.
10. Never delete records; mark them as superseded or archived when necessary.

---

# File Update Rules

* Append new entries; do not rewrite historical records.
* Use unique identifiers (e.g., `TASK-0001`, `EMAIL-0001`, `CHAT-0001`) that increment sequentially.
* Maintain chronological order.
* Use Markdown headings for easy navigation.
* Keep one financial-year file per record type, creating a new file at the start of each financial year.

---

# Compliance

All employees, including AI agents, are responsible for maintaining their own records. Managers will verify compliance during reviews, and the Documentation Engineer will conduct periodic audits to ensure completeness and consistency.
