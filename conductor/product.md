# AbhiHub Product Definition

## Vision
AbhiHub is an all-in-one academic resources platform designed for engineering and university students, offering seamless access to previous year question papers (PYQs), study notes, practical materials, AI-powered question answering, and student community tools.

## Core Features
1. **Academic Repository**: Categorized storage and viewing of PYQs, lecture notes, syllabus, and assignment solutions.
2. **Authentication**: Supabase-powered Email and Google OAuth authentication with persistent sessions.
3. **AI Study Assistant**: AI-powered paper question-answering with multi-model fallback and document vision capabilities.
4. **Student Profiles & Gamification**: Referral credits, leaderboards, profile customization, and study pass tracking.
5. **Real-time Notifications & Chat**: Web Push notifications and real-time Socket.IO chat.
6. **Analytics & Insights**: Privacy-compliant Google Analytics 4 and Supabase server-side event tracking.
