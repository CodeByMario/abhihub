iOS Notifications Setup

On iPhone/iPad (iOS 16.4+), Apple requires web apps to be added to your Home Screen to deliver push notifications when the browser is closed.
1. Tap the Share button (square with arrow) at the bottom of Safari.
2. Select "Add to Home Screen".
3. Open AbhiHub from your Home Screen and enable push notifications!
