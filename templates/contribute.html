Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about publishing and monetizing your notes.

What academic materials can I upload to AbhiHub?
You can upload university previous year question papers (PYQs), handwritten lecture notes, unit-wise study guides, practical manuals, and solved assignments in PDF or clean high-resolution image format.
Can I sell handwritten notes on AbhiHub?
Eligible creators can upload original handwritten notes and previous year question papers as paid documents, set a price during upload, and receive creator earnings from completed purchases. Verified view earnings may also apply under the wallet policy.
What benefits do I get for contributing on AbhiHub?
Active uploaders advance through rank tiers (Explorer, Member, Contributor, Power Contributor, Community Leader) to unlock higher daily upload limits, faster search quotas, reduced ad density, and contributor profile badges.
What are the quality requirements for uploading study material?
Uploads must be original or open academic material with a clear title (min 5 characters), selected college and subject, accurate semester/year tags, and a helpful description of at least 50 words to pass automated quality indexing.
