Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about contributing study resources.

What academic materials can I upload to AbhiHub?

You can upload university previous year question papers (PYQs), handwritten lecture notes, unit-wise study guides, practical manuals, and solved assignments in PDF or clear image format.

What benefits do I get for contributing on AbhiHub?

Active uploaders advance through rank tiers (Explorer, Member, Contributor, Power Contributor, Community Leader) to unlock higher daily upload limits, faster search quotas, reduced ad density, and contributor profile badges.

Are there direct cash payouts for uploads?

No. Currently, AbhiHub rewards contributors through gamified rank tiers, platform privileges, and community reputation. There are no cash payouts at this stage.

What are the quality requirements for uploading study material?

Uploads must be original or open academic material with a clear title (min 5 characters), selected college and subject, accurate semester/year tags, and a helpful description of at least 50 words.
