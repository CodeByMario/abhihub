\section{A simple pilot}
1. Choose 3--5 original resources and the audience each one serves.\\
2. Confirm ownership, price, sample pages, listing details and commercial terms.\\
3. Organise the channel around your exam, branch, subject or semester.\\
4. Share the channel with your students; review visits, orders and questions.\par\vspace{4mm}
{\small\color{muted}\textbf{An additional distribution channel.} Keep your existing website, course and community; use AbhiHub to give selected resources a focused home.}\par\vfill\hrule\vspace{2mm}
